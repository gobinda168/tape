Scope: the Tape extension surfaces (popup, in-page recording bar + camera bubble, permissions page, library/review page) and the server share page. Visitor mode: Operate.

Audience/job: someone mid-task who needs to show something on screen and paste a link seconds after stopping. Task frequency: several times a day, one take.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A recording is an instant print. You stop, it develops, it is yours. Refuses the category default of a grey dashboard with a purple record button and a grid of identical video cards.

OWN-WORLD: Instant-camera body and print. Chrome (popup, recording bar) is charcoal camera-body plastic #1D1E20 with the five-band spectrum stripe (red #E5402F, orange #F08A24, yellow #F2C230, green #3FA15A, blue #2F78C4) used only as state: red = recording, yellow = paused/working, orange = saved on this computer, blue = has a share link, green = setup step done; the full stripe appears only as a short mark beside the wordmark. Library ground is a cool grey tabletop #E4E5E7 (dark: #141516); prints are cool white #FBFBFA frames with a thick bottom margin that holds the title and destination marks. Geist for everything, Geist Mono for timers and sizes; radius 6px for controls, 3px for prints, full round for the camera bubble and record button.

STORY: The user picks what to record, records, stops. The print develops while it saves to their destination; the link lands on the clipboard. They see where every recording lives and can send it again.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Library page: 56px top bar (Tape mark with spectrum stripe left, destination status chips right, Settings), then left the newest print large (player inside the frame, title editable in the bottom margin, destination rows with state and Copy link), right a column of older prints in a tight two-up contact grid. Popup: three mode segments, source, camera/mic selects with live preview + level, one red Start button with full-round ends and a label (a bare round button read as ambiguous next to the mode controls).

FORM: Instant photograph (Polaroid-style print), position 3 of 7 on the grounded list (slate, cassette J-card, instant print, on-air tally, self-storage door, card catalog, railway clock). Seed key 09d30c69.
Raise (from airport wayfinding, declined): every surface shows only the next decision; the recording bar carries only stop, pause, restart, cancel.
Raise (from ticket wallet, declined): saved states stamp and stay; a failed upload shows as a struck row with Retry, never disappears.
Raise (from design annual, declined): one family carries display and caption alike.
Signature move: develop. A new print starts murky (blurred, low-contrast, warm cast) and develops to full image as the save completes; progress is the development, not a spinner.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
